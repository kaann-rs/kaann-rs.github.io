> Skeleton. One heading per thing learned; delete the ones you do not need.

## Something that surprised me

**What I expected.** The assumption you went in with.

**What actually happened.** The measurement, the error message, the profile —
whatever contradicted it.

**What I do now.** The rule you follow from here on.

## A trade-off I had to make

Two options, what each cost, and why the one you picked is right *for this
workload* rather than in general.

## Something I got wrong first

The version that did not work and the reason it did not. This is the part worth
writing: it is the one a reader cannot get from the source.
