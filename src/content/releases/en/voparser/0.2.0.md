### Added
- `vp_query` with tag, class, id, descendant, child, attribute and
  `:nth-child` selectors.
- Fuzzing harness and a starting corpus under `tests/fuzz/`.

### Changed
- The tokeniser reads through a table instead of a switch, which cut about 18%
  off the parse loop on the crawl corpus.
- Attribute names are compared without allocating.

### Fixed
- A document ending mid-tag no longer reads one byte past the buffer. Found by
  the fuzzer on its second day.
